# GDPR DPA

DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM This Data Protection Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Agreement between the customer identified in the Agreement ("Customer") and NAVER Cloud (the "Company"). This DPA applies to the extent that (i) the Company processes Personal Data of Customer’s customers (end-users) or its employees on behalf of Customer in the course of providing Services (as defined below) and (ii) the Agreement expressly incorporates this DPA by reference. This DPA shall not apply where the Company is the Controller. The terms used in this Addendum shall have the meanings set forth in this Addendum. Capitalized terms not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning given to them in the Agreement. Except as modified below, the terms of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

1.

Definitions

1.1 In this Addendum, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

1.1.1

"Agreement” means the agreement (including Service Terms) between Customer and the Company for the provision of the Services to Customer;

1.1.2

"Data Protection Laws" means all data protection and privacy laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including, where applicable, EU Data Protection Laws;

1.1.3

"EU Data Protection Laws" means (i) prior to 25 May 2018, Directive 95/46/EC

of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of individuals with

regard to the processing of Personal Data and on the free movement of such data

(“Directive”); and (ii) on and after 25 May 2018, Regulation 2016/679 of the

European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of natural persons with

regard to the processing of Personal Data and on the free movement of such data

(General Data Protection Regulation) (“GDPR”);

1.1.4

"Services" means [Naver Cloud Platform] service or customer support services provided by the Company to Customer pursuant to the Agreement;

1.1.5

"Subprocessor" means any Processor engaged by the Company or any member of its group of companies that processes Personal Data pursuant to the Agreement. Sub-processors may include third parties or any member of the Company’s group of companies;

1.1.6

"EU Standard Contractual Clauses" means the contractual clauses set out in Annex 2, amended as indicated (in brackets and italics) therein;

1.2 The terms, "Commission", "Controller", "Data Subject", "Member State", “Personal Data”, "Personal Data Breach", "Processing", "Processor" and "Supervisory Authority" shall have the same meaning as in the GDPR, and their cognate terms shall be construed accordingly.

2.

Processing of Personal Data

2.1 Customer is the Controller of all Personal Data processed pursuant to the Agreement and agrees to the Processing of Personal Data by the Company as set out in this DPA. Customer appoints

the Company as Processor to process the above Personal Data. If there are other Controllers, Customer will identify and inform the Company of any such other Controllers prior to providing their Personal Data.

2.2 Customer will, in its use of the Services, comply with its obligations under Data Protection Laws in respect to its processing of Personal Data and any processing instructions it delivers to the Company. Customer represents that it has all rights and authorizations necessary for the Company to process Personal Data pursuant to the Agreement.

2.3 The Company is the Processor who processes Personal Data according to Customer’s instructions which are defined by the Agreement. Customer and the Company agree that the Agreement is the complete and final instructions to the Company in relation to the processing of Personal Data. Processing any Personal Data outside the scope of the Agreement will require prior written agreement between Customer and the Company.

2.4 Customer may provide instructions which are not defined in the Agreement only when such instructions are legally required under Data Protection Laws. If the Company reasonably finds such instructions illegal or inappropriate under Data Protection Laws, the Company will inform Customer without undue delay and may suspend processing under such instructions until Customer has modified or confirmed the lawfulness of such instructions in writing.

2.5 Annex 1 to this Addendum sets out certain information (i.e. list of categories of Data Subjects, types of Personal Data, processing activities, duration of the Processing, nature and purpose of the Processing) regarding the Processors' Processing of the Personal Data. The Company may make reasonable amendments to Annex 1 by prior written notice to Customer. Nothing in Annex 1 confers any right or imposes any obligation on any party to this Addendum.

3.

Security and Confidentiality

3.1 The Company will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures stipulated in Article 32 of the GDPR to protect against Personal Data Breaches and to preserve the security and confidentiality of Personal Data processed by the Company on behalf of Customer in the provision of the Services (“Security Measures”). The Security Measures are subject to technical progress and development. The Company may update or modify the Security Measures from time to time provided that such updates and modifications do not result in the degradation of the overall security of the Services purchased by the Customer. Customer confirms that the Security Measures provide an appropriate level of protection against the risks in relation to the Processing of Personal Data.

3.2 The Company restricts its personnel from processing Personal Data without authorization (unless required by Data Protection Laws) and will ensure that any person authorized by the Company to process Personal Data is subject to an obligation of confidentiality.

3.3 Customer is responsible for using and configuring the Services in a manner which enables Customer to comply with Data Protection Laws, including implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures.

4.

Subprocessing

4.1 The Company may, with the prior consent of Customer or a separate written agreement, engage Subprocessors to provide certain parts of the Services. The Company will be responsible for any acts, errors or omissions of its Subprocessors that cause the Company to breach any of the Company’s obligations under this DPA.

4.2 The Company will have a written agreement with each Subprocessor that obligates such Subprocessor to protect Personal Data in a manner substantially similar to the standards set forth in the Agreement.

5.

Data Subject Rights

5.1 Taking into account the nature of the Processing, the Company shall assist Customer by implementing appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfillment of the Customer' obligations, as reasonably understood by the Company, to respond to requests regarding Data Subject rights under the Data Protection Laws.

5.2 The Company shall:

5.2.1

notify Customer without undue delay if the Company or any Subprocessor receives a request from a Data Subject under any Data Protection Laws in respect of Personal Data; and

5.2.2

ensure that the Company or any Subprocessor do not respond to that request except on the documented instructions of Customer unless the Company or any Subprocessor are required to respond to such request by Data Protection Laws.

6.

Personal Data Breach

6.1 The Company shall notify Customer without undue delay upon the Company or any Subprocessor becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data, providing the Company with sufficient information to allow Customer to meet any obligations to report or inform Data Subjects of the Personal Data Breach under the Data Protection Laws.

6.2 The Company shall co-operate with Customer and take such reasonable commercial steps as are directed by Customer to assist in the investigation, mitigation and remediation of each such Personal Data Breach.

7.

Data Protection Impact Assessment and Prior Consultation

The Company shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer with any data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with Supervising Authorities or other competent data privacy authorities, which the Company reasonably considers to be required of Customer by Article 35 or 36 of the GDPR or equivalent provisions of any other Data Protection Laws.

8.

Destruction or Return of Personal Data

Upon expiration or termination of the Agreement, the Company will destroy or return to Customer all Personal Data in the Company’s possession as provided in the Agreement unless the Company is required by applicable laws to retain some or all of the Personal Data. The terms of this DPA will continue to apply to such Personal Data.

9.

Audit Rights

The Company audits its compliance against data protection and information security standards on a regular basis. Such audits are conducted by the Company’s internal audit team or by third party auditors engaged by the Company. The specific audits, and the data protection and information security certifications the Company has achieved, will necessarily vary depending upon the nature of the Services in question. Upon Customer's written request, and subject to obligations of confidentiality, the Company will make available to Customer a summary of its

most recent relevant audit report and/or other documentation reasonably required by Customer which the Company makes generally available to its customers, so that Customer can verify the Company's compliance with this DPA.

10. Data Transfers 10.1 The Company may transfer and process Personal Data to and in other locations around the world where the Company or its Sub-processors maintain data processing operations as necessary to provide the Services as set forth in the Agreement. Such data transfer includes transfer of Personal Data from the European Economic Area ("EEA") to a country not recognized by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of protection for Personal Data. 10.2 By agreeing to this DPA, Customer established in EU is entering into the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as referred to in Annex 2 in this DPA, with the the Company as Processor established outside either the EEA or countries considered by the European Commission to have adequate protection. 10.3 In case Customer stipulated in Section 10.2 notifies the Company another Controller or the Company engages a new Subprocessor, each party will suggest another party without undue delay amendment of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses stipulated in Section 10.2 or entering a separate EU Standard Contractual Clause.

11. General Terms 11.1 Nothing in this DPA reduces the Company's obligations under the Agreement in relation to the protection of Personal Data or permits the Company to process (or permit the processing of) Personal Data in a manner which is prohibited by the Agreement. 11.2 In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this DPA and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (if applicable) shall prevail. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and any privacy-related provisions in the Agreement, the terms of this DPA will prevail. 11.3 The Company may modify the terms of this DPA as provided in the Agreement, in circumstances such as (i) if required to do so by a Supervisory Authority or other government or regulatory entity, (ii) if necessary to comply with Data Protection Laws, or (iii) to implement or adhere to EU Standard Contractual Clauses, approved codes of conduct or certifications, binding corporate rules, or other compliance mechanisms, which may be permitted under Data Protection Law. Supplemental terms may be added as an Annex to this DPA where such terms only apply to the processing of Personal Data under the Data Protection Laws of specific countries or jurisdictions. The Company will provide notice of such changes to Customer, and the modified DPA will become effective, in accordance with the terms of the Agreement or as otherwise provided on the Company’s website if not specified in the Agreement. 11.4 Should any provision of this DPA be invalid or unenforceable, then the remainder of this DPA shall remain valid and in force. The invalid or unenforceable provision shall be either (i) amended as necessary to ensure its validity and enforceability, while preserving the parties’ intentions as closely as possible or, if this is not possible, (ii) construed in a manner as if the invalid or unenforceable part had never been contained therein.
